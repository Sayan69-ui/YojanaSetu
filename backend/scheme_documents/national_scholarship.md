# National Scholarship Portal (NSP) Central Sector Schemes

## 1. Overview and Purpose
The National Scholarship Portal (NSP) is the digital gateway managed by the National Informatics Centre (NIC) and Ministry of Electronics & IT under Digital India. It converges various scholarship schemes offered by Central Government Ministries (Ministry of Education, Ministry of Social Justice, Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Ministry of Minority Affairs, etc.) for school, college, and university students.

Official Portal: https://scholarships.gov.in
Helpdesk: 0120-6619540 / Email: helpdesk@nsp.gov.in

## 2. Prominent Schemes Hosted on NSP
1. **Central Sector Scheme of Scholarship for College and University Students (CSSS)**:
   - For students pursuing regular degree/diploma courses in recognized colleges.
   - ₹12,000 per annum for graduation (first 3 years) and ₹20,000 per annum for post-graduation.
2. **Post Matric Scholarship Schemes for SC / ST / OBC / Minorities**:
   - Covers compulsory non-refundable fees plus monthly maintenance allowance for class 11, 12, ITI, Polytechnic, UG, and PG programs.
3. **National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMMSS)**:
   - ₹12,000 per year for meritorious students from class 9 to 12 from low-income backgrounds.
4. **Pragati and Saksham Scholarships (AICTE)**:
   - ₹50,000 per year for girl students and specially-abled students pursuing technical degree/diploma courses.

## 3. General Eligibility Criteria
- Must be a regular, full-time student enrolled in a recognized institution registered with AISHE/DISE code.
- Minimum Academic Merit: Usually minimum 50% to 60% marks in the qualifying board or university examination.
- Family Annual Income Limits:
  - For CSSS: Up to ₹4,50,000 per annum.
  - For Post-Matric SC/ST/OBC/Minority: Up to ₹2,50,000 per annum.
- Student must not be receiving any other government scholarship.
- Valid Aadhaar Card or Aadhaar Enrollment ID seeded with a functional bank account.

## 4. Key Features & Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
- Single online application for multiple central and state scholarships.
- Transparent Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) directly into student’s bank account using Aadhaar payment bridge, eliminating leaks and delays.
- Real-time application tracking and biometric authentication via One-Time Registration (OTR).

## 5. Mandatory Documents for Application
- Student Aadhaar Card / OTR Number.
- Marksheet of previous qualifying exam.
- Family Income Certificate issued by Revenue Authority (Tehsildar/SDM).
- Caste/Category Certificate (where applicable).
- Bonafide Student Certificate from current School/College.
- Fee receipt of the current academic year.
- Bank account passbook in student's name.
