# Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) - Housing for All

## 1. Overview
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY) envisions providing pucca (permanent) houses equipped with basic amenities (potable water, sanitation, clean cooking gas, electricity) to all eligible rural (PMAY-Gramin) and urban (PMAY-Urban) homeless families and those living in dilapidated houses.

Official Portal: https://pmaymis.gov.in (PMAY-U) / https://pmayg.nic.in (PMAY-G)
Helpline: 1800-11-6163 / 1800-11-3377

## 2. Scheme Components and Subsidies

### A. PMAY-Gramin (Rural)
- Financial grant of ₹1,20,000 per unit in plain areas.
- Financial grant of ₹1,30,000 per unit in hilly, northeastern, and difficult terrains / IAP districts.
- Additional ₹12,000 for toilet construction in convergence with Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen).
- 90/95 days of unskilled labor wage under MGNREGA (worth approx. ₹25,000).

### B. PMAY-Urban (PMAY-U 2.0)
- Beneficiary-Led Construction (BLC): Direct financial grant of ₹1.5 lakh from central government + state top-up for construction of new house or enhancement of existing kutcha house.
- Credit Linked Subsidy Scheme (CLSS) / Interest Subsidy: Up to ₹2.67 lakh interest subsidy on housing loans for Economically Weaker Section (EWS) and Low-Income Group (LIG) families.
- Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP): Financial assistance for houses built with private or public developers.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- The beneficiary family should NOT own a pucca house in their name or in the name of any member of their family in any part of India.
- EWS category: Annual household income up to ₹3,00,000.
- LIG category: Annual household income between ₹3,00,001 and ₹6,00,000.
- The house must ideally be registered in the name of the female head of the household or in joint ownership with husband.
- Beneficiary must not have received central assistance under any prior housing scheme.

## 4. Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card of all family members.
- Bank account details (Passbook copy with IFSC).
- Income Certificate / BPL Certificate / Ration Card.
- Land title deed / patta / affidavit proving ownership of land where house will be built.
- Passport-sized photographs.
