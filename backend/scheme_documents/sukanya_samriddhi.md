# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

## 1. Overview and Philosophy
Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSA) is a government-backed small deposit savings scheme launched under the "Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao" campaign by the Ministry of Finance. It helps parents build an education and marriage corpus for girl children with sovereign safety and highest government-guaranteed interest rates.

Official Portal: https://www.indiapost.gov.in / Reserve Bank of India
Toll-Free Customer Service: 1800 266 6868

## 2. Key Financial Highlights
- **High Interest Rate**: Currently pays ~8.2% per annum (reviewed quarterly by the Government of India), which is higher than standard Fixed Deposits.
- **Triple Tax Exemption (EEE Status)**:
  - Deposit amount qualifies for tax deduction under Section 80C up to ₹1.5 lakh per financial year.
  - The interest accrued is completely tax-free.
  - The entire maturity proceeds are 100% tax-free.
- **Deposit Flexibility**:
  - Minimum deposit: ₹250 per financial year.
  - Maximum deposit: ₹1,50,000 per financial year.
  - Can be deposited in cash, cheque, DD, or online via net banking / IPPB.
- **Deposit Period**: Deposits can be made up to 15 years from the date of account opening.
- **Maturity Period**: The account matures on completion of 21 years from the date of opening or upon the girl child's marriage after attaining 18 years of age.

## 3. Eligibility Rules
- The account can only be opened in the name of a girl child from her birth until she reaches 10 years of age.
- Only one account is permitted per girl child.
- Maximum of two accounts per family (for two girl children). Exception: An account can be opened for more than two girl children in case of twin/triplet girls born in the first or second order of birth.
- The account is operated by the natural or legal guardian until the girl child attains the age of 18.

## 4. Premature & Partial Withdrawal Rules
- Partial withdrawal of up to 50% of the balance standing at the end of preceding financial year is permitted after the girl reaches 18 years of age or passes 10th standard, specifically for higher education expenses.
- Normal closure permitted upon marriage of the girl after attaining 18 years.

## 5. How to Open an SSY Account
Parents can open an account at any Post Office branch or authorized public/private commercial banks (SBI, PNB, BoB, ICICI, etc.).
Documents required:
- SSY Account Opening Form.
- Birth Certificate of the girl child.
- Aadhaar Card / PAN Card of the parent or guardian.
- Address Proof of parent/guardian.
