# Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY)

## 1. Overview and Goal
Launched under the Ministry of Finance, the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY) facilitates institutional credit up to ₹20 lakh to non-corporate, non-farm small/micro enterprises. It aims to "fund the unfunded" by promoting self-employment, small shops, artisan businesses, and manufacturing micro-units.

Official Portal: https://www.mudra.org.in / https://www.udyamimitra.in
Helpline: 1800 180 1111 / 1800 11 0001

## 2. Loan Categories and Loan Limits
MUDRA loans are divided into three tiers depending on the stage of enterprise development:
1. **Shishu Category**:
   - Covers loans up to ₹50,000.
   - Designed for nascent stage entrepreneurs, small vendors, local repair shops, and home-based ventures.
2. **Kishore Category**:
   - Covers loans above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5,00,000.
   - Designed for growing businesses requiring working capital, machinery, or raw materials.
3. **Tarun Category**:
   - Covers loans above ₹5,00,000 and up to ₹10,00,000 (and extended up to ₹20 lakh for entrepreneurs who have successfully repaid prior Tarun loans under Mudra 2.0).
   - Designed for established micro and small enterprises expanding production or retail capabilities.

## 3. Key Features and Advantages
- **Collateral-Free**: No collateral or mortgage or third-party guarantee is required.
- **Zero Processing Fees**: Typically waived for Shishu category loans.
- **Mudra Debit Card**: Borrowers receive a RuPay debit card linked to their Mudra working capital account for flexible withdrawals as and when needed, saving on interest.
- Available through Public Sector Banks, Private Banks, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs), Small Finance Banks, and Microfinance Institutions (MFIs).

## 4. Eligibility Requirements
- Any Indian citizen aged 18 to 65 years who has a viable business plan for a non-farm income-generating activity in manufacturing, processing, trading, or service sector.
- The applicant must NOT be a defaulter with any bank or financial institution.

## 5. Application Procedure and Documents
Apply online on the Udyamimitra portal (https://www.udyamimitra.in) or submit application directly at any commercial bank branch.
Required documents:
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN).
- Proof of residence (Electricity bill, Ration card).
- Business address & registration proof (Udyam Registration certificate, Shop & Establishment license).
- Last 6 months bank statement.
- Business plan/project report with estimation of machinery/equipment purchases.
