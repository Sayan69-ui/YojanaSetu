# PM Vishwakarma Scheme

## 1. Overview and Mission
PM Vishwakarma is a Central Sector Scheme launched by the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) to give holistic support to traditional artisans and craftspeople who work with their hands and traditional tools (Guru-Shishya parampara).

Official Portal: https://pmvishwakarma.gov.in
Helpline: 18002677777 / 011-23061500

## 2. 18 Eligible Traditional Trades
The scheme specifically covers 18 traditional professions:
1. Carpenter (Suthar)
2. Boat Maker
3. Armourer
4. Blacksmith (Lohar)
5. Hammer and Tool Kit Maker
6. Locksmith
7. Sculptor (Moortikar / stone carver / stone breaker)
8. Goldsmith (Sonar)
9. Potter (Kumhaar)
10. Cobbler (Charmakar / Shoesmith / Footwear artisan)
11. Mason (Raajmistri)
12. Basket / Mat / Broom Maker / Coir Weaver
13. Doll & Toy Maker (Traditional)
14. Barber (Naai)
15. Garland maker (Malakaar)
16. Washerman (Dhobi)
17. Tailor (Darzi)
18. Fishing Net Maker

## 3. Comprehensive Benefits Package
1. **Recognition & Identity**: PM Vishwakarma Certificate and digital ID Card.
2. **Skill Upgradation**:
   - Basic Training of 5 to 7 days (40 hours).
   - Advanced Training of 15 days or more (120 hours).
   - Daily training stipend of ₹500 per day during the training period.
3. **Toolkit Incentive**: A grant/e-voucher of ₹15,000 for purchasing modern high-quality tools upon starting skill training.
4. **Enterprise Development Credit (Loans)**:
   - Collateral-free enterprise credit at an ultra-low concessional interest rate of only 5% (with subvention of 8% paid by Ministry).
   - 1st Tranche: Up to ₹1,00,000 (repayable in 18 months).
   - 2nd Tranche: Up to ₹2,00,000 (repayable in 30 months) for beneficiaries who maintain standard loan accounts and adopt digital transactions.
5. **Digital Transaction Incentive**: ₹1 per transaction up to 100 transactions per month.
6. **Marketing Support**: Quality certification, branding, onboarding onto GeM (Government e-Marketplace) and e-commerce platforms.

## 4. Eligibility Requirements
- An artisan or craftsperson working with hands and tools engaged in one of the 18 specified trades.
- Minimum age of 18 years on the date of application.
- Limited to one member of a family.
- The beneficiary should not have availed loans under similar credit-based schemes (PMEGP, Mudra, PM SVANidhi) in the past 5 years.
- No government employees or their family members are eligible.

## 5. Enrollment Process
Free enrollment via Aadhaar biometric authentication at any Common Service Centre (CSC) followed by 3-stage verification (Gram Panchayat / ULB level, District Implementation Committee, and Screening Committee).
