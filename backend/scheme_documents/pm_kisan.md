# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

## 1. Overview and Objectives
Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN) is a Central Sector Scheme launched by the Government of India to augment the financial needs of landholding farmer families in procuring various inputs related to agriculture and allied activities as well as domestic needs. Under the scheme, 100% financial assistance is provided directly by the Central Government.

Official Portal: https://pmkisan.gov.in
Helpline Numbers: 155261 / 011-24300606 / Toll Free: 1800-115-526

## 2. Financial Benefits and Disbursement Mode
- An amount of ₹6,000 per year is released directly into the bank accounts of the eligible farmer families.
- The amount is distributed in three equal four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 each:
  - Period 1: April to July
  - Period 2: August to November
  - Period 3: December to March
- The payment is transferred directly via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to the Aadhaar-seeded bank account of the beneficiary.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- All landholding farmer families having cultivable landholding in their names are eligible.
- A 'Farmer Family' is defined as a family consisting of husband, wife, and minor children.
- Both small and marginal farmers as well as other cultivable landholders are eligible as per updated guidelines.

## 4. Exclusion Criteria (Who is NOT Eligible?)
The following categories of beneficiaries of higher economic status are NOT eligible:
1. All Institutional Landholders.
2. Farmer families in which one or more of its members belong to following categories:
   - Former and present holders of constitutional posts.
   - Former and present Ministers/State Ministers and former/present Members of Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha/State Legislative Assemblies/State Legislative Councils.
   - Former and present Mayors of Municipal Corporations and Chairpersons of District Panchayats.
   - All serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government Ministries, Offices, Departments, PSUs, and autonomous institutions (excluding Multi-Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D employees).
   - All superannuated/retired pensioners whose monthly pension is ₹10,000 or more (excluding Group D employees).
   - All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
   - Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects registered with professional bodies and carrying out profession.

## 5. Mandatory Documents and Registration Process
To register or continue receiving benefits under PM-KISAN, the farmer must provide:
- Aadhaar Card (Mandatory).
- Proof of ownership of agricultural land (Land Records / RoR / Khasra-Khatauni).
- Aadhaar-linked Savings Bank Account details with IFSC code.
- Active Mobile Number for OTP authentication.
- Completion of mandatory e-KYC on the PM-KISAN portal (via OTP or Biometric at CSC centers).

Farmers can register online via 'Farmer Corner' on https://pmkisan.gov.in or through their nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) or State Agriculture Department Nodal Officer.
