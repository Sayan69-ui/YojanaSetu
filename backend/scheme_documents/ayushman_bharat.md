# Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)

## 1. Overview and Scope
Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY is the flagship national public health insurance scheme of the Government of India, administered by the National Health Authority (NHA). It is designed to provide financial protection to approximately 55 crore vulnerable citizens against catastrophic health expenditures.

Official Portal: https://pmjay.gov.in
National Helpline: 14555 / Toll-Free: 1800-111-565

## 2. Key Benefits and Coverage
- Health cover of up to ₹5,00,000 (Rupees Five Lakh) per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.
- Completely cashless and paperless access to healthcare services at all empanelled public and private hospitals across India.
- No cap on family size, age, or gender.
- All pre-existing conditions are covered from day one of enrollment.
- Covers up to 3 days of pre-hospitalization (diagnostics, consultation) and 15 days of post-hospitalization care (medicines, check-ups).
- Over 1,949 medical procedures including cardiology, oncology, neurosurgery, orthopedics, and pediatric surgery are covered.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
PM-JAY is entitlement-based. Families do not need to enroll separately if they belong to identified categories:
- Rural Households: Families categorized under deprivation criteria (D1 to D7) based on Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 (e.g., households with one room with kuccha walls/roof, female-headed households with no adult male member, households with disabled members, SC/ST households, landless manual casual laborers).
- Urban Households: 11 defined occupational categories of unorganized workers (rag pickers, domestic workers, street vendors, cobblers, construction workers, transport workers, sweepers, artisans, etc.).
- Active Ration Card Holders under National Food Security Act (NFSA) as notified by various State Governments.
- Recently expanded to cover all senior citizens aged 70 and above, regardless of income level (under the Ayushman Vay Vandana expansion).

## 4. How to Check Eligibility and Get the Ayushman Card
1. Visit https://pmjay.gov.in or https://beneficiary.nha.gov.in.
2. Enter mobile number and OTP to search by Aadhaar Number, Ration Card (NFSA), or Family ID.
3. If eligible, generate and download the digital Ayushman Card / PVC Ayushman Card at any empanelled hospital or Common Service Centre (CSC).
4. Carry Aadhaar Card and Ration Card to the hospital's 'Ayushman Mitra' desk for instant verification and admission.
