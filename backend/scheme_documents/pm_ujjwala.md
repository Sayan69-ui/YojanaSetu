# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY / Ujjwala 2.0)

## 1. Overview
Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana was launched by the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MoPNG) with the motto "Swachh Indhan, Behtar Jeevan" (Clean Fuel, Better Life). The scheme empowers rural and economically disadvantaged women by providing deposit-free LPG connections, eliminating health hazards caused by hazardous biomass cooking fuels.

Official Portal: https://www.pmuy.gov.in
Helpline: 1800-266-6696 / Emergency LPG Helpline: 1906

## 2. Benefits Provided under Ujjwala 2.0
- **Deposit-Free LPG Connection**: Security deposit for 14.2 kg cylinder and pressure regulator is funded entirely by the Central Government.
- **First Refill and Hotplate (Stove) Free**: Beneficiaries receive their very first LPG cylinder refill and a standard ISI-marked two-burner stove free of charge.
- **Direct Targeted Subsidy**: Current targeted subsidy of ₹300 per 14.2 kg cylinder credited directly into the woman's bank account for up to 12 refills per year.
- **Migrant Friendly**: Under Ujjwala 2.0, migrant workers are not required to submit proof of permanent address; a self-declaration is accepted for family composition and local residence.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- The applicant must be an adult woman (at least 18 years of age).
- Must belong to an economically weaker household (BPL / SECC deprivation list / SC / ST / Most Backward Classes / forest dwellers / island inhabitants).
- No other LPG connection must exist in the same household under any Oil Marketing Company (IOCL / BPCL / HPCL).
- Annual household income must be within poverty thresholds defined by state/UT.

## 4. Required Documents
- Aadhaar Card of the female applicant as proof of identity and address.
- Ration Card issued by State Government / document confirming family composition.
- Aadhaar of all adult family members residing together.
- Active Bank Account Number and IFSC Code for DBT subsidy.
- Self-declaration for migrant families.

## 5. How to Apply
Applications can be submitted online at https://www.pmuy.gov.in by choosing an LPG distributor (Indane, Bharatgas, or HP Gas), or in person at the local LPG gas agency.
