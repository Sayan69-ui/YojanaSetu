# Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

## 1. Overview and Significance
Atal Pension Yojana (APY) is a flagship social security scheme administered by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) under the Ministry of Finance. It provides a guaranteed defined monthly pension to citizens working in the unorganized sector to prevent financial vulnerability during old age.

Official Portal: https://www.npscra.nsdl.co.in / https://www.pfrda.org.in
Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 110 069

## 2. Salient Benefits and Pension Tiers
- **Guaranteed Monthly Pension**: Subscribers can choose a fixed monthly pension of ₹1,000, ₹2,000, ₹3,000, ₹4,000, or ₹5,000 to be paid on reaching 60 years of age until death.
- **Spousal Guarantee**: After the subscriber passes away, the exact same monthly pension amount continues to be paid to the surviving spouse for the rest of their life.
- **Nominee Inheritance**: Upon the demise of both the subscriber and spouse, the entire accumulated pension wealth (corpus) is handed back to the registered nominee.
- **Affordable Contributions**: Monthly contribution amounts depend on the joining age and desired pension slab. For example, an 18-year-old needs to contribute only ₹42 per month to secure a ₹1,000/month pension, or ₹210 per month for a ₹5,000/month lifelong pension.

## 3. Eligibility Conditions
- Any Indian citizen aged between 18 and 40 years.
- The applicant must hold a Savings Bank Account with a commercial bank or post office.
- **Income Tax Rule (Effective 1st Oct 2022)**: Any citizen who is or has been an income tax payer under the Income Tax Act is NOT eligible to join APY.

## 4. Operational Guidelines
- Contributions are automatically debited from the subscriber's bank account on a monthly, quarterly, or half-yearly basis through the auto-debit facility.
- Premature exit before age 60 is permitted only under exceptional circumstances like terminal illness or death of the subscriber.
- The subscriber receives a Permanent Retirement Account Number (PRAN) and e-PRAN card online.

## 5. Required Documents and Enrollment
- Aadhaar Card.
- Active Savings Bank Account details.
- Active Mobile number.
Enrollment can be done online through Internet Banking or by visiting any branch of your bank or post office.
