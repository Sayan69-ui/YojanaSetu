# PM SVANidhi (PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi)

## 1. Context and Objective
PM SVANidhi is a Central Sector micro-credit scheme launched by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) to provide affordable, collateral-free working capital loans to urban and peri-urban street vendors to help them resume and scale their businesses.

Official Portal: https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in
National Toll-Free Helpline: 1800 11 1979

## 2. Graduated Loan Tranches and Incentives
The scheme offers an escalating credit limit that rewards disciplined digital and financial behavior:
1. **First Tranche**:
   - Working capital loan of up to ₹10,000.
   - 1-year repayment tenure in convenient monthly installments.
2. **Second Tranche**:
   - Enhanced working capital loan of up to ₹20,000 upon timely or early repayment of the 1st loan.
3. **Third Tranche**:
   - Substantial working capital loan of up to ₹50,000 upon successful repayment of the 2nd loan.
4. **7% Interest Subsidy**:
   - Borrowers who repay loans on time receive an interest subsidy of 7% per annum directly credited into their bank accounts quarterly.
   - No penalty for early repayment!
5. **Cashback for Digital Transactions**:
   - Monthly cashback up to ₹100 (₹1,200 annually) for vendors who adopt digital UPI payments (receiving at least 50 eligible digital receipts per month).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Available to all street vendors, hawkers, thela-walas, and rehri-patri owners who were engaged in vending in urban and semi-urban areas.
- Beneficiary must hold either:
  - Certificate of Vending (CoV) or Identity Card issued by Urban Local Bodies (ULB / Municipality), OR
  - Identified in the town vendor survey with a Letter of Recommendation (LoR) from the Town Vending Committee (TVC).
- Peri-urban and rural vendors vending in geographical limits of ULBs are also covered.

## 4. Why This Scheme Stands Out
- **Zero Collateral**: No security or mortgage required.
- **Credit Score Building**: Helps unorganized street vendors enter the formal banking system and establish a credit score (CIBIL) for future larger commercial loans.
- **Family Profiling (SVANidhi se Samriddhi)**: Facilitates socio-economic profiling of vendor families to link them to 8 other welfare schemes (PMJJBY, PMSBY, PM-JAY, One Nation One Ration Card, etc.).

## 5. Required Documents and Application Process
Apply online at https://pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in or via the PM SVANidhi mobile app, or through local Common Service Centres (CSCs) and bank branches.
Required documents:
- Aadhaar Card.
- Vending Certificate / ULB ID card / Letter of Recommendation (LoR).
- Bank Account details.
- Mobile number linked to Aadhaar.
